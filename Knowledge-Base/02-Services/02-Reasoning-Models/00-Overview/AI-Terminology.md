# AI Reasoning Models — Glossary of Terminology

An alphabetized technical glossary detailing concepts, reward systems, and training mechanics of AI reasoning models.

---

## Glossary of Terms

### **Chain-of-Thought (CoT)**
A sequential list of logical, mathematical, or conversational steps generated internally by the model to plan its response before returning a final answer.

### **Distilled Reasoning**
The process of capturing raw chain-of-thought traces generated by large frontier reasoning models (e.g. DeepSeek-R1 671B) and using them to fine-tune smaller, lightweight models (e.g. Llama 8B/70B) to transfer specific logical planning capabilities.

### **GRPO (Group Relative Policy Optimization)**
A memory-efficient reinforcement learning algorithm developed by DeepSeek. It bypasses traditional actor-critic value networks by generating a group of outputs for each input prompt, and evaluating each candidate relative to the group average.

### **Hidden Thinking Tokens**
Tokens generated by the model during its reasoning phase that represent the intermediate chain of thought. These tokens are billed at standard output rates, but are typically hidden from final client interfaces.

### **MCTS (Monte Carlo Tree Search)**
A heuristic search algorithm used to navigate decision trees by running random simulations. Combined with deep reinforcement learning, it allows models to evaluate logical paths (as seen in early search models like AlphaGo).

### **ORM (Outcome Reward Model)**
A reinforcement learning reward system that grades only the final completed output of a generation, without evaluating the validity of individual steps.

### **PRM (Process Reward Model)**
An advanced reinforcement learning reward system that grades every intermediate reasoning step within the model's chain of thought, encouraging logical correctness at each stage.

### **System 2 Thinking**
A cognitive psychology term referring to slow, deliberate, logical, and analytical thinking. In AI, this refers to reasoning models scaling test-time compute.

### **Test-Time Compute Scaling**
Scaling the computation time and tokens generated *during inference* (generating longer thinking sequences) rather than scaling training parameter size.
